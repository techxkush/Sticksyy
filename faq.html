NEED A LITTLE HELP?

Frequently asked.

The useful bits about picking, paying for, and receiving your stickers.

What am I buying?

Each listing is an A4 sticker sheet. Check the product page for its design, category, and current price.

How do I place an order?

Add stickers to your bag, continue to checkout, and enter your delivery details. Checkout will show the amount and UPI payment instructions.

How do I pay?

Pay using the UPI QR or UPI option shown at checkout. Submit the payer name and UTR/reference number so the store can check the transfer.

When is my order confirmed?

Submitting a UTR does not confirm payment. The store manually checks the payment in its receiving UPI/bank account. Your order is confirmed only after that check.

Where can I see my order?

Open your profile menu and choose “Your orders.” Order history currently appears only in the browser where you placed the order.

How do shipping charges work?

Checkout shows the shipping charge for your current cart before you pay. The store currently offers free shipping on orders above ₹399.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Contact the store as soon as possible at hello@sticksy.in with your order reference. Changes depend on whether the order has already been packed or shipped.

What if my order arrives damaged or is incorrect?

Contact hello@sticksy.in with your order reference and a photo of the issue so the store can help.

How do sign-in options work?

You can sign in with Google or with an email address and password. Create an account first if you are new to email sign-in.

How do I save favourites?

Tap the heart on a product card. Favourites are currently saved in your browser, so they may not sync across devices.

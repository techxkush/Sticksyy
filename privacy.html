THE IMPORTANT LITTLE DETAILS

Privacy Policy.

We collect only what we need to run your account and get your sticker order to you.

Last updated: November 2026

Information we collect

When you create an account, Supabase Auth processes the sign-in information you choose to use, such as your email address, phone number, or Google profile details. When you place an order, we collect your name, phone number, email, delivery address, PIN code, products, and order total.

If you pay by UPI, we ask for the payer name and UTR/reference number so we can manually check the transfer. We do not ask for or store your UPI PIN, bank password, card number, or OTP.

How we use it

We use this information to sign you in, process and deliver orders, check submitted UPI references, respond to support requests, and keep the store secure. We do not sell personal information.

Where it is stored

Account and order data are handled by Supabase services configured for this store. Cart and favourites may also be stored in your browser. Information saved only in your browser may not follow you to another device or browser.

Sharing and service providers

We use service providers needed to operate the store, including Supabase for authentication and order records, Google when you choose Google sign-in, and your UPI app or bank when you pay. Their own privacy terms may apply.

Retention and your choices

We keep order information for as long as needed to manage the order, support, and applicable record-keeping. You can ask to access, correct, or delete account information by contacting hello@sticksy.in. Some order records may need to be retained for operational or legal reasons.

Children and updates

This store is intended for general shoppers and is not designed to collect information from children under 18 without a parent or guardian. We may update this notice as the store changes; the latest version will be posted here.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@sticksy.in.
